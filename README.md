# Day 1 - CRM Basics

## 1. What is CRM?

CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.

It is a software system used by companies to manage:
- Customer information
- Sales activities
- Communication history
- Support requests
- Business workflows

CRM helps organizations improve customer relationships and increase sales efficiency.

Examples of CRM platforms:
- Salesforce
- HubSpot
- Zoho CRM
- Microsoft Dynamics 365

---

## 2. Why Companies Use Salesforce

Companies use Salesforce because it helps them:

### Centralize Customer Data
All customer details are stored in one place.

### Improve Sales Tracking
Sales teams can track leads, opportunities, and deals easily.

### Automate Business Processes
Tasks like emails, follow-ups, and approvals can be automated.

### Better Customer Support
Support teams can resolve customer issues faster.

### Reporting & Analytics
Managers can generate reports and dashboards for decision-making.

### Cloud-Based Access
Employees can access Salesforce from anywhere.

---

## 3. Salesforce Objects Explanation

### Account
An Account represents a company or organization.

#### Example:
- Infosys
- TCS
- Amazon

Accounts store:
- Company Name
- Industry
- Address
- Phone Number

---

### Contact
A Contact represents a person associated with an Account.

#### Example:
- Ravi Kumar (Manager at Infosys)
- John Smith (HR at Amazon)

Contacts store:
- Name
- Email
- Phone Number
- Job Title

---

### Opportunity
An Opportunity represents a potential sales deal.

#### Example:
- Software License Deal
- CRM Implementation Project

Opportunities help track:
- Deal Amount
- Sales Stage
- Closing Date
- Probability of Winning

---

## 4. Real-World Mapping

| Real World | Salesforce Object |
|------------|------------------|
| Company | Account |
| Employee/Customer | Contact |
| Business Deal | Opportunity |

### Example Scenario

# Conclusion

CRM systems help businesses manage customers, improve sales, and automate workflows. Salesforce is one of the most widely used CRM platforms in the world.
