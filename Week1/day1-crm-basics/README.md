# Day 1 - CRM Basics

## What is CRM?

CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.

It is a system used by companies to manage customer data, sales activities, communication, support, and business relationships in one platform.

CRM helps businesses:
- Store customer information
- Track sales and leads
- Improve customer service
- Increase productivity
- Automate business processes

---

## Why Companies Use Salesforce

Salesforce is one of the world's leading CRM platforms.

Companies use Salesforce because it:
- Helps manage customer relationships efficiently
- Automates sales and service processes
- Provides cloud-based access from anywhere
- Offers real-time reports and dashboards
- Improves team collaboration
- Supports customization and app development

Salesforce is widely used in:
- Sales
- Customer Support
- Marketing
- Healthcare
- Banking
- Real Estate

---

## Salesforce Objects Explanation

### 1. Account

An Account represents a company, organization, or customer business.

Example:
- Infosys
- TCS
- Amazon

Accounts store business-related information like:
- Company Name
- Industry
- Phone Number
- Address

---

### 2. Contact

A Contact represents a person associated with an Account.

Example:
- Employee
- Customer
- Manager

Contacts store information like:
- Name
- Email
- Phone Number
- Job Title

Example:
- Ravi Kumar works at Infosys

Here:
- Infosys = Account
- Ravi Kumar = Contact

---

### 3. Opportunity

An Opportunity represents a potential sales deal.

It helps companies track:
- Sales stage
- Deal amount
- Expected revenue
- Closing date

Example:
- Selling software worth ₹5,00,000 to a company

Opportunity stages include:
- Prospecting
- Qualification
- Proposal
- Negotiation
- Closed Won

---

## Real-World Mapping

| Real World Example | Salesforce Object |
|--------------------|------------------|
| ABC Company | Account |
| John (Manager at ABC Company) | Contact |
| Software Deal with ABC Company | Opportunity |

Example Scenario:
- A company named ABC Technologies wants to purchase CRM software.
- The company is stored as an Account.
- The sales manager from the company is stored as a Contact.
- The software purchase deal is stored as an Opportunity.
